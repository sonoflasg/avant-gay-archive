A two-part essay by [[Brian Droitcour]], director of Outland, published on Outland's Substack. Part 1 (23 Dec 2025) is subtitled "Brian Droitcour on NFTs that turn the logic of post-internet art inside out." Part 2 (3 Feb 2026) is subtitled "Brian Droitcour on art that treats the rituals of collectability as its medium."

### Part 1: Drifella III and the installation view

- Droitcour opens with [[Drifella]] and [[Drifella 2]] by [[Evil Biscuit]]: collections that "use the familiar format of the profile-picture NFT, or PFP, while also breaking it apart." In his words, "The image gets congested, swollen with traits."
- In [[Drifella III]] (2025), "this excess expands into space. Each work shows a triptych installed in a room." He reads it "as an image of categories dissolving. Collectibles melt into art; art melts into collectibles."
- He contrasts it with post-internet art of the early 2010s, which used the installation shot to gain art-world visibility. Drifella III borrows the same format "to very different ends."
- His conclusion: "for artists like Evil Biscuit, the art world is no longer the primary horizon of value."

### Part 2: collectability as medium

- Droitcour places Biscuit alongside the artists Magid and Dawood. He calls Biscuit "an artist without an art-world background who produces conceptually ambitious work by pushing collectible logic to its limits." He adds that "All three iterations of his Drifella project emerge from a milieu described as schizocollage or avant-gay NFTs" (see [[Schizocollage]], [[Avant Gay]]).
- [[Parker Ito]] is his example of an artist who came from net art and post-internet contexts: "His NFT collections released in 2025 translate that impulse into schizocollage PFPs that cannibalize art history."
- He discusses [[Profile Pictures]], made with [[Xanthrax]], which traces the three-quarter PFP portrait back to Raphael's *Pope Julius II*. In his reading, it "cites art history not to reclaim its authority but to expose how its compositional logics circulate on NFT markets."

### How it relates to other sources

Droitcour's other essays in this archive are [[Seams and Synthesis]], [[Two Trends in NFT Art]] and [[Artmaxxing The New Bad Image]]. Here he calls the milieu "schizocollage or avant-gay NFTs" without claiming either term (see [[Schizocollage]] on who coined it).
