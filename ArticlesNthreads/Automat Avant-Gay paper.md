*Avant/Gay: An Artistic Movement of Internet Culture* is a long research paper by [[Giannis Sourdis]], published by [[Automat]] on 9 November 2026. Automat announced it the same evening (10:49pm AEDT) as its most extensive paper to date, “part research, part preservation”: https://x.com/Automatdotart/status/2119759317196808399

[[Giannis Sourdis]] (X: @Greekdx) quoted that announcement at 11:19pm AEDT and described a project that began in late June 2026, took more than four months, and grew into 44 unique interviews (calls or written questions over DMs) after he found history disappearing from deleted chats, dead sites, and vanished accounts. He frames the scene as first taking shape on Ethereum, then becoming established on [[Solana]], and later returning to Ethereum. He also said the paper would continue to receive updates: https://x.com/Greekdx/status/2119766740930334977

The live essay is a static, illustrated HTML article (print/PDF, with a project finder over the appendix), not a community archive. It argues that [[Avant Gay]] can be studied as a retrospective umbrella for related practices earlier called Avant NFT, [[Cuckcore]], and Gay NFT, without treating those phases as identical or closing membership. An editorial note states that the work was human-led and AI-supported, with factual verification left to the author.

This archive treats the paper as one major, attributed perspective — not as a last word. [[SonOfLasG]]’s earlier essays, including [[Avant Gay]], [[Iridium Pilled Notes on the Avant Gay]], and [[All Roads Lead to MiFella]], and his trait-genealogy diagram, are cited in the paper’s references. He was thanked for writing and documentation but was not among the 44 interviewees. The paper discusses participant-led records such as cuckcore.de, [[jpegs.cool]], and [[13-33 - 1333 Covenant]]; it does not mention this archive.
