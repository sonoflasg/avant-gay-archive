An interview with [[Jared Madere]] by Katie Chiou, part of the "IN CONVERSATION WITH" series published by Archetype. Published on 14 May 2024, three days before [[Galerie Yeche Lange]] opened its New York space.

### What it records

- **The team.** The introduction calls Madere "the Co-founder of Galerie Yeche Lange." It lists the rest of the team as "Milo Conroy, Wretched Worm, Miles Peyton, Supermetal Bosch, Anastasios Karnazes, and Hikkimourning" (see [[Milo Conroy]], [[Wretched Worm]], [[Miles Peyton]], [[Supermetal Bosch]]). It says Yeche has collaborated with "little swag world, Tojiba CPU Corp, and more" ([[Little Swag World]], [[Tojiba]]). The physical gallery was opening "on May 17, 2024" (see [[Haircuts]]).
- **On [[Drifella 2]].** "In the gallery, we're going to have a massive DriFella painting. To me, DriFellas represent some of the most radical image-making I've seen in the last 15 to 20 years." He describes the method: "a few thousand image components that are pseudo-randomly collaged together according to predefined weights. The resulting images feel like violent onslaughts of stickers piled on top of each other." See [[Evil Biscuit]] and [[trait-maxxing]].
- **Why open a physical space.** "This crypto world has been opaque to the traditional art world... Making the fruits of these cultures visible to audiences beyond collectors has been a huge driver for us to bring Yeche Lange into the form of a physical gallery as a bridge." He also mentions a planned Yeche Lange archive platform "cataloging every collection these artists have done."
- **Pie Keys.** The interview describes "Galerie Yeche Lange Pie Keys" (2022) as a membership-key collection. Madere says it "minted out almost instantly" and "basically funded the gallery treasury for two years."

### Where it differs from other sources

This team list is longer than Bankless's of Feb 2024 (see [[The Rise of Avant-Garde NFTs]]), which names Madere, Conroy, Wretched Worm and Peyton. It also differs from Le Random's later lists. See [[Galerie Yeche Lange]].
