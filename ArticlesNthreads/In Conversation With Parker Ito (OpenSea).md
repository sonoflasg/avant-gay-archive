An interview with [[Parker Ito]] by Hannah Vanek on the OpenSea blog, published on 1 Dec 2025. It ran ahead of [[Profile Pictures]], made with [[Xanthrax]], which minted on 2 Dec 2025 on OpenSea with Shape.

### What it says about the scene

- **How Ito arrived.** "A couple of years ago, in 2023, I was asked to do an NFT project... When I started this new project, I started seeing a lot of Solana NFTs pop up on my timeline... For the last year and a half, I've been involved primarily with the Avant Gay Solana scene." See [[Avant Gay]].
- **Net art.** "When I discovered artists on Solana making NFTs, it reminded me of those early net art days... It felt like coming full circle."
- **Derivatives.** On Profile Pictures, Ito says he was "thinking about that lineage in parallel with derivative collections on Solana. I don't mean 'derivative' in a negative sense. I mean derivative as in collections that reuse and reinterpret earlier collections, a lot of the time using the same assets."

He makes the same point about derivatives ten months later in [[Parker Ito on the Dumb Truth of Contemporary Art]].
