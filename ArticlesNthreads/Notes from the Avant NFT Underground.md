“Notes from the Avant NFT Underground” is an essay by [[GardenParty85]] published by [[Right Click Save]]. The page is dated 3 November 2025. The Automat paper’s reference list gives 4 December 2025; this archive uses the date on the page. The essay thanks Giannis Sourdis. It is one of the earliest long histories of the scene written by a participant.

### What it covers

- **Definition.** It calls Avant NFTs “aesthetically aware, guided by humor and the absurd,” and compares them to Fluxus in being “definable according to their conceptual approach and network rather than a single style.” It calls the scene “an international digital art movement where persona is medium.”
- **Names.** It credits [[Supermetal Bosch]] with first coining “Avant NFT” in a 25 Jan 2022 post. It says the “Gay NFTs” name came from an exchange between [[New Art Program]] and [[Tojiba Brand Manager]]. See [[Avant NFT]] for the competing accounts.
- **[[Spiky DJ]].** A project by the collective FPBJPC, with the tag line “never minting out.” Spiky headphone traits recur in [[MiFella]] (dated 2022 in the essay), [[Drifella]] (2023) and [[Little Swag World]] (2024).
- **[[Galerie Yeche Lange]].** An online-only gallery launched in 2022. Its team was curators [[Milo Conroy]], [[Jared Madere]] and Wretched Wurm ([[Wretched Worm]]), editor Anastasios Karnazes and developer [[Miles Peyton]], and its works were minted on Ethereum. In spring 2024 its “format and personnel changed” and it opened a New York space for physical work. “In early 2025, Yeche built a launchpad for Solana NFTs called VVV.so,” which “is not curated.”
- **[[Milady]].** Launched in August 2021, “through the artwork of [[Henry Sprite]] and the performative character of its leader,” Charlotte Fang. It records the spring 2022 controversy.
- **[[MiFella]].** “A project about Milady,” but “an exercise in experimental aesthetics.” “Although Mifella is a single account, there are many Mifella artists and anyone can claim Mifella and launch a Mifella collection.” Later projects form the [[Cuckcore]] subset. The essay summarises the lore: MiFella is born, dies, is fused with a dead Pokémon to make Drifella, and dissolves into a [[chair]].
- **Method.** [[trait-maxxing]] or [[Schizocollage]], made with [[Hashlips]], read as a “technological spin on Surrealist automatism.”
- **Other projects.** [[Cigawrette Packs]] (2022) by Pappachaga, a literary project with more than 90 fictional cigarette brands and alternate-reality gaming. Super Metal Mons! (2021), GAN-generated from images of keshi miniatures, later cast in porcelain and turned into a chess-like game. Little Swag World, built from “photoshopped assets and Stable Diffusion.”
- **Economy.** fairybaby420’s memecoin work, MiFella community airdrop farming, and [[Earl @earl16o1|Earl]]’s candle-chart oil paintings and “trader fiction.”
