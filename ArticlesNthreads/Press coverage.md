A list of short press items, newsletter notes and forum posts that mention the [[Avant Gay]] scene or its artists without being long enough for their own entry. Each item was read in full on the date above; quotes are exact. Dates are the outlet’s own publication dates. Longer pieces have their own entries: see [[Wet Paint Avant-NFT movement]], [[The Rise of Avant-Garde NFTs]], [[In Conversation With Jared Madere (Archetype)]], [[Notes from the Avant NFT Underground]], [[The Vulgar Image]], [[In Conversation With Parker Ito (OpenSea)]], [[After the Art World]], [[Seams and Synthesis]], [[Artmaxxing The New Bad Image]], [[Two Trends in NFT Art]], [[The Pack Is the Painting]], [[Parker Ito on the Dumb Truth of Contemporary Art]] and [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]].

### 2022

- **Spike, 8 Jun 2022.** Dean Kissick, "The Downward Spiral: Taste, Figures, Images." Kissick recalls listening to "an episode of Contain about NFTs" in which "the founders of the Tojiba CPU Corp project spoke about" the 10,000-run PFP series that dominated the market. It is an early art-press mention of [[Tojiba]]. https://www.spikeartmagazine.com/articles/the-downward-spiral-taste-figures-images

### 2025

- **cult.now (cult DAO), dated 15 Sep 2025.** Review of [[One of Us Is Real and It's Not You]] at [[Galerie Yeche Lange]], credited to "-sys(cry)." It calls [[Supermetal Bosch]] and [[Evil Biscuit]] "two artists forged in the furnace of the Avant NFT underground," and says: "If Bosch builds worlds, Evil Biscuit burns them down." The page also promotes a token, which is not repeated here. https://www.cult.now/mag/one-of-us-is-real-and-its-not-you-supermetal-bosch-evil-biscuits
- **Emergent Magazine, exhibition page.** One Of Us Is Real And It's Not You, Galerie Yeche Lange, New York, "June 26, 2025 - September 14, 2025." Installation views and works including Evil Biscuit's *Drifella 3 triptych* (2025) and Bosch's porcelain pieces. https://www.emergentmag.com/exhibitions/one-of-us-is-real-and-its-not-you-at-galerie-yeche-lange
- **Right Click News (Right Click Save newsletter), 7 Nov 2025, Louis Jebb.** Headlined "Lumen Prize, Avant NFTs, Art Basel go big on Digital Art." It features GardenParty85's history: "A core member of the Avant NFT community traces a history of the grassroots movement." https://rightclicksave.beehiiv.com/p/right-click-news-lumen-prize-avant-nfts-art-basel-go-big-on-digital-art
- **Outland, "Context Creator," 12 Nov 2025.** An unsigned newsletter item on [[Heavy Liquid Graphic]]: "The NFT marketplace vvv.so launched in early 2025 and has already cultivated an active community of artists and collectors around a style known as gay NFTs or schizocollage—labels that remain intentionally fluid and informal." It notes that the collection "stands out for its use of negative space." It also discusses [[Drilady]] and [[Drifella III]]. https://outlandart.substack.com/p/context-creator
- **Artnet News, 21 Dec 2025.** Andrew Russeth, "Pour One Out for the Galleries That Closed in 2025." Among promising new New York galleries: "There's Galerie Yeche Lange, which has been doing outrageous shows in an office at the tip of Manhattan (though it's suddenly gone dark)." https://news.artnet.com/art-world/contemporary-art-galleries-2025-2731623
- **Right Click Save, "Right Click Save's Top 10 Stories in 2025," 23 Dec 2025.** GardenParty85's "Notes from the Avant NFT Underground" is No. 5. https://www.rightclicksave.com/article/right-click-saves-top-10-stories-in-2025-artificial-intelligence-digital-art-nfts

### 2026

- **Right Click Save, "A-Z of Digital Art 2026," January 2026.** The entry "A is for … Avant NFTs" says "2025 cemented the Avant NFT scene as a hive of grassroots, natively digital, and often provocative activity." It quotes [[GardenParty85]] ("it has been great to see so many new people come to appreciate the Avant/Gay NFT scene") and [[Peter Bauman]] ("More experimental groups like the Avant NFT scene on Solana and Glitch Residency thrived"). https://www.rightclicksave.com/article/a-z-of-digital-art-2026-art-and-technology-a-to-z
- **Outland, "Context Creator: The Platform Collapse Edition," 12 Feb 2026.** Quotes the [[vvv.so]] manifesto posted to X: "We're not trying to rebuild the 2021 market. We don't want that market back." https://outlandart.substack.com/p/context-creator-the-platform-collapse
- **Spike #87 (Spring 2026), "Everything's Computer."** Spike's shop sold "Evil Biscuit, »Spike TCG«, 2026": "A limited-edition series of 91 acetone-washed, reprinted, holofoil trading cards, designed on the occasion of" that issue. https://spikeartmagazine.com/shop/evil-biscuit-spike-trading-cards-2026
- **Outland, "Four Questions for Net Art History," 11 Jun 2026, [[Brian Droitcour]].** "The communities that produced this work—surf clubs, the Jogging, the avant-gay NFT milieu— aren't incidental context. They constitute the work." https://outlandart.substack.com/p/four-questions-for-net-art-history
- **Outland, "Context Creator: Highlights Edition," 19 Jun 2026.** On [[Projections]] by [[Tojiba Brand Manager]]: "a mash-up of installation view and NFT collection. Across 1,102 images, the same scene repeats: a projection on a wall in a darkened gallery." https://outlandart.substack.com/p/context-creator-highlights-edition
- **Right Click Save forum, "Trading Card Aesthetics," [[Brian Droitcour]], about August 2026.** The forum shows only relative dates; the page's earliest timestamp is 17 Aug 2026. "The format has been embraced by emerging artists like terrorism and Evil Biscuit." He groups Biscuit with "others who are parodying it and/or messing with its formal structure (Rare Pepes, Evil Biscuit)." https://www.rightclicksave.com/post/44/trading-card-aesthetics
- **Wallpaper\*, Frieze London 2026 live updates, 15 Oct 2026, Hannah Silver.** On [[Parker Ito]] at Rose Easton: "part mechanical, part craft, it's akin to entering a fantasy-filled dystopia." https://www.wallpaper.com/art/live/frieze-london-2026-live-updates-from-the-wallpaper-team
- **Outland, "Coding a New Gift Economy," 10 Nov 2026, [[Brian Droitcour]].** At Art Blocks Marfa Weekend, an artist called Rax ran "a guerrilla trading-card show" with "packs from Evil Biscuit's Card NFT in a binder." Droitcour writes that [[Card NFT]] "draws on the established practice of hand-embellishing trading cards." https://outlandart.substack.com/p/coding-a-new-gift-economy
