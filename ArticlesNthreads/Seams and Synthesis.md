“Seams and Synthesis: Schizocollage and AI Aesthetics” is an essay by [[Brian Droitcour]] published by [[Le Random]] on 2 February 2026. Droitcour argues that art made with NFTs works between two aesthetics. [[Schizocollage]] “foregrounds visible seams and difference.” Generative AI “synthesizes difference into coherent wholes.”

### Points relevant to this archive

- It reads [[Evil Biscuit]]’s [[Drifella]] collages as images that “insist on their own stitched-together-ness.” It says the original Drifella collection “was introduced in 2023,” with the legend of [[Dratini]] resurrected with the soul of [[MiFella]].
- It places [[Supermetal Bosch]]’s Mons and [[Little Swag World]], with Ann Hirsch and Maya Man’s Ugly Bitches and Little Darlings, as projects that “sit adjacent to (but not inside) the avant-NFT milieu.” These are “not trait-based.” Other sources treat Little Swag World as central to the Solana scene; see that entry.
- Little Swag World began from mood-board collages of fashion fragments that the model then smoothed into whole figures. GardenParty85 mapped its references on X.
- It discusses Yeche Lange’s summer 2025 two-person show [[One of Us Is Real and It's Not You]], which placed Bosch’s Mons and Little Swag World with Biscuit’s Drifella works.
- Droitcour uses “schizocollage” as an established term. He does not claim to have coined it.
