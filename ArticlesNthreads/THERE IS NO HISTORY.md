“THERE IS NO HISTORY” is an essay by [[lowbie]] published in the texts section of [[vvv.so]] on 23 August 2025. It describes the “‘Avant’/’Gay’ scene” as “a collection of artists making works mainly on Solana, held together by a vague ‘sensibility’ and taste.” It notes more than 100 collections and press including Spike features, Brian Droitcour’s essays and Parker Ito interviews.

### What it covers

- **Entry through [[Milady]].** It reads Milady as “‘Performance Art’ for an online generation.” It describes the scene as “a forked path away from Remilia,” with “no central, consensus ‘group’.”
- **[[MiFella]].** “‘Mifella is about Milady’, reads the collections description.” The essay describes its punk and noise references and calls it “an answer to a feeling … deep in the ‘trenches’.” It says [[MiFella 2]] “took traits from 10 artists in the scene.”
- **[[Drifella]].** Created by [[Evil Biscuit]], it is “one of the earlier examples of ‘traitmaxxing’.” The essay defines [[trait-maxxing]] as “10s of layers and 100s-1000s of traits compiled together by the art software Hashlips.” The sequel, [[Drifella 2]], was “minted on Solana.”
- **The Jofella Saga.** A 13-chapter fan fiction based on the NFTs, written by the artist [[Earl @earl16o1|Earl]], “almost 200 pages long” as a PDF. It draws on the Tojiba world of [[Tojiba Brand Manager]] and Tojiba CEO.
- **Footnote on names.** “Gay” was coined “in a series of tweets between Tojiba and New Art Program.” On “Avant,” see [[Avant NFT]]: Madere told lowbie it was “supposedly” coined by Spiky DJ or Milady/Charlotte Fang.
- It quotes [[Parker Ito]]’s Archetype interview: NFTs are “the first time we have a true memetic art.”
