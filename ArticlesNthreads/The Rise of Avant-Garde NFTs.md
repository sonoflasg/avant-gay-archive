An issue of *Metaversal*, the Bankless newsletter by William M. Peaster, published on 15 Feb 2024: "The Rise of Avant-Garde NFTs." The project section is credited to "Bankless writer: David C." It is one of the earliest pieces of trade-press coverage of the scene found so far. It predates Artnet's April 2024 column ([[Wet Paint Avant-NFT movement]]).

### What it records

- **Framing.** It traces the trend to [[Milady]]: "when Miladies arrived in August 2021 and dropped the first major weirdness bomb on the NFT ecosystem's PFP sector." It calls "the realm of avant-garde NFTs" "still niche and fairly amorphous."
- **[[Supermetal Bosch]].** Known for "his 2021 collection, SupermetalMons," which he developed "into a physical chess-like board game." It says [[Little Swag World]] "launched in December 2023" alongside a Solana token. The newsletter's token and marketplace plans are not repeated here.
- **[[Tojiba]].** "Launched in early 2022, Tojiba CPU Corp exemplified the essence of the first avant-garde NFT wave on Solana." It covers Toji 100, a Discord "simulating life working in a factory," a coin called Ijot, and [[disc buddies]], "a companion collection to the main Toji 100."
- **[[Galerie Yeche Lange]].** "born in June 2022, comes from the minds of curators Jared Madere, Milo Conroy, Wretched Worm, and developer Miles Peyton" (see [[Jared Madere]], [[Milo Conroy]], [[Wretched Worm]], [[Miles Peyton]]). It names the inaugural show "Tournament for Swollen Hearts Round Un," with Amalia Ulman and [[Spiky DJ]], and an auction event, "The Yechelles, School of Truth."
- **Schizoposters,** a Milady-derived collection "created by Twitter anons apparently unaffiliated with the Milady team."

### Where it differs from other sources

- **Little Swag World's date.** This newsletter says December 2023. The Automat paper registers 15 Jan 2024 (see [[Little Swag World]]).
- **Yeche Lange's start.** "June 2022" here. Artnet (Apr 2024) says Madere had run it online "for the past three years," which would mean about 2021. GardenParty85 says 2022.
