“The Vulgar Image” is an essay by critic Dean Kissick, first printed in Spike issue 84 (Summer 2025, “Vulgarity”) and published online. It surveys an AI-era visual culture of excess.

In its list of examples Kissick names “the subcultural ‘avant’ or ‘gay NFT’ movement of self-consciously esoteric, visually overcomplicated and relatively financially worthless collections, with MiFella’s abject ‘cuckcore’ aesthetic at its heart.” It is one of the first art-magazine descriptions of the scene, and it reproduces an image from [[MiFella 2]].

Kissick’s framing puts [[MiFella]] and [[Cuckcore]] at the centre of the movement. Le Random’s definition centres VVV instead. The Automat paper treats Cuckcore as one phase under a wider umbrella. See [[Avant Gay]] and [[Avant NFT]]. In April 2024 Kissick hosted the Super Metal Mons tournament at his gallery, Earth, described in [[Wet Paint Avant-NFT movement]].
