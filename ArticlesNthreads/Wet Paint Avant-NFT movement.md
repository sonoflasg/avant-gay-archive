On 11 April 2024, Annie Armstrong’s Wet Paint gossip column in Artnet News ran a section headed “Introducing: the ‘Avant-NFT’ movement.” It is the earliest mainstream press coverage of the scene found so far.

### What it records

- **A soft launch at Earth.** The previous weekend, artists and others gathered at critic Dean Kissick’s Lower East Side salon and gallery, Earth, for a tournament of Super Metal Mons, the chess-like board game by [[Supermetal Bosch]]. Armstrong describes the event as a soft launch for [[Galerie Yeche Lange]].
- **Yeche Lange before New York.** The gallery “currently exists only on Discord” and was “cofounded by” [[Jared Madere]]. Madere had been “operating Galerie Yeche Lange online” “for the past three years.” *Inference:* that puts the online start around 2021, earlier than the 2022 given by GardenParty85.
- **11 Broadway.** The physical space was to open “next month at 11 Broadway.” The Automat paper dates the opening show, [[Haircuts]], from 17 May 2024.
- **The term.** The gallery’s specialty is “a movement that the artist terms ‘avant NFT’.” Madere: “‘Avant NFTs’ are about images, and pushing images to an extreme place.” Kissick praises Madere’s optimism about NFTs.
- **Artists.** An illustration credits work by Wretched Worm, Supermetal Bosch, Nomemene, Miles Peyton, Tojiba CPU Corp, Jim Spindle, Evil Biscuit, Jared Madere, Spiky DJ, Sam Hains and earl16o1.
- **Bed Stuy Love Affair.** Madere ran this roving gallery out of an RV in New York “about a decade ago.”
