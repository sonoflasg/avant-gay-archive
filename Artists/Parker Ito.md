[[Parker Ito]] (X: @CreamyDreamy) is a Post-Internet painter and image-maker whose on-chain work became a central point of contact between an earlier internet-art generation and the [[Solana]] [[Avant Gay]] scene. This note is compiled by SonOfLasG from public sources. It is not a biography in Ito’s voice.

### Public outline (Automat paper)

The [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]] (Giannis Sourdis, 9 Nov 2026) places Ito as a conceptual bridge from Post-Internet image circulation to this later on-chain scene, not as its founder. According to that paper’s account of two interviews with Ito (13 and 24 Aug 2026), his first NFT collection, [[ParkerIto.net]], was released on Ethereum on 22 Aug 2024, with a token-to-painting exchange; his sustained encounter with the Solana scene began in early 2024, when works such as [[Drifella 2]], [[Self-Driving Megaworms]], and [[Horses?]] first felt aesthetically specific to him. Later collaborations include [[Heavy Liquid Graphic]], [[The Night Parade of 33 Demons]], and [[111k]].

### Accounts that differ

**On whether a 2024 London exhibition explains his route into the scene.** The paper records *[[Sprite x Parker]]*, a show with [[Henry Sprite]] presented by Zien at The Handbag Factory, 23–26 May 2024. In the same account, Ito says he did not attend and recalls having little contact with Sprite. The paper therefore treats the exhibition as more important to later interpretation than to Ito’s own path onto [[Solana]].

**On whether [[Drilady]] widened the audience.** One early participant cited in the paper saw Drilady as carrying the [[Drifella]] line to a different public. Ito, in the same study, is more cautious: he locates a rise in attention around his Verse project and [[Drifella III]], and notes that he was one of relatively few people discussing Gay NFT in interviews outside the scene. The paper leaves that shift as something he participated in, not as an effect that can be assigned to Drilady alone.

**On labels.** Ito’s public X account has repeatedly circulated other people’s “avant gay” framing, including a 7 Sep 2025 repost that “avant/gay is the new wave, traitmaxxing is the meta”: https://x.com/CreamyDreamy/status/1964816455125565846. That is circulation, not a manifesto. In the Automat interviews, several artists including [[Evil Biscuit]] are described as ambivalent about the movement label itself; this note does not collapse those positions into a single “Ito thesis.”

### Interview in the Automat paper

[[Parker Ito]] was interviewed by [[Giannis Sourdis]] for the [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]] (two interviews, 13 and 24 Aug 2026). The paper’s public References section records that those interviews were used for: his earlier interest in NFTs as a medium; entry into the [[Solana]] Gay NFT scene through [[Drifella 2]], [[Self-Driving Megaworms]], and [[Horses?]]; and later works including [[Pilgrim’s Living Room Crucifixion]], [[Drilady]], and related Ethereum releases.

This paragraph records only what is published in the References. It is not a transcript.


### In 111k (public credits, still partial)

[[Evil Biscuit]], on 10 Nov 2026, said Ito had made “multiple sub blocks” of “vast scenes and large paintings,” expanding on [[Horses?7]] and [[Pilgrim’s Living Room Crucifixion]], one of them depicting the realms Drifella and [[Drilady]] come from. He did not give those blocks’ 111k titles, and this page does not assign unsigned titles to Ito. Ito’s own 9 Nov 2026, 10:16am AEDT post names a “Mind Body Spirit / tarot card system” inside [[111k]] that did not match a live block title in a 14 Nov metadata snapshot: https://x.com/CreamyDreamy/status/2119569827761414456. His public posts also describe relic and “god crate” mint mechanics; see the [[111k]] chapter.
