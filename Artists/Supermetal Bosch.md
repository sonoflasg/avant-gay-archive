[[Supermetal Bosch]] (X: @supermetalx) is an artist in the early Avant NFT circle and later the [[Solana]] [[Avant Gay]] scene, known for the Mons body of work and for [[Little Swag World]]. This file was empty in the vault. The existing [[Little Swag World]] note (a Grok draft, flagged needs-sources) already names him as the author of that collection and uses the handle supermetalx. This entry is compiled by SonOfLasG from that pointer, from the [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]], and from contemporaneous public X posts. It is not a biography in Bosch’s voice.

### Public outline

The paper dates [[Super Metal Mons!]] to August 2021 on Ethereum (144 works), an early GAN project built from photographs of toys and miniatures, and [[Super Metal Mons!!]] to March 2022 (777 works). It also records later digital-physical work: ceramics, a Mons game credited in an interview with [[Ivan]] to Bosch and [[Turtle]], and Solana collections including [[Little Swag World]] (with [[Autotune Friends]], Golira, and Turtle among those Bosch credited in a process thread cited by the paper). Related stubs in this archive now include [[Milady GAN]], [[EMOJIPACK]], [[Gushies]], and [[Little Swag World - HEXP]].

### Accounts that differ

**On Remilia’s role in Super Metal Mons!!.** The paper sets two descriptions side by side. Bosch, in his 11 Aug 2026 interview, characterizes [[Remilia]]’s contribution chiefly as technical infrastructure — the contract and website — that he did not yet know how to build. [[Charlotte Fang]], in a public account cited by the paper, gives Remilia a larger role: after seeing the earlier Mons work, Fang says he proposed adapting it to a randomly minted generative PFP format, after which Remilia built the site and contract and advised on metadata. Both accounts are recorded here; this page does not pick one.

**On who coined “avant NFT.”** Public posts from the period show Bosch and neighbors using the phrase, and they show other artists crediting him for it. On 27 Jan 2022, 7:16am AEDT, [[Spiky DJ]] posted “s/o @supermetalx for coining the term avant NFT”: https://x.com/spiky_dj_/status/1486432931811889156. Bosch had already been posting under the term, including “avant nft season soon bois” (https://x.com/supermetalx/status/1482820845873549315) and “avant nft starter pack” (https://x.com/supermetalx/status/1486199049111982082). The Automat paper also cites a 16 Jan 2022 Fang reply, “it was bosch,” which could not be retrieved as a live post during this compilation, and an earlier 20 Nov 2021 Bosch post using the phrase; those remain citations of the paper until live URLs are confirmed. The paper additionally records an anonymous early participant questioning whether linguistic coinage and the social construction of the category are the same thing. As the paper puts it, these accounts need not be reconciled into a single version.

### Interview in the Automat paper

[[Supermetal Bosch]] was interviewed by [[Giannis Sourdis]] for the [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]] (interview, 11 Aug 2026). The paper’s public References section records that this interview was used for: the origins of [[Super Metal Mons!]] and [[Super Metal Mons!!]]; the second collection’s relationship to [[Remilia]]; Bosch’s ceramic practice and the Mons game; later projects including Gushies, [[EMOJIPACK]], Super Sofubi, and [[Little Swag World]]; and AI-assisted character work across digital and physical formats.

This paragraph records only what is published in the References. It is not a transcript.
