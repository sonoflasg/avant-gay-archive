[[Tojiba]] (Tojiba CPU Corp) is the fictional collaborative company associated with early collections such as Toji 100, [[disc buddies]], and Toji-A. [[Tojiba Brand Manager]] is the individual artist identity that operates within and beyond that company.

This distinction is the one [[Tojiba Brand Manager]] gives in a written interview used by the [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]] (July 2026): official Tojiba NFT collections are a short list, while later works such as [[Horses?]], [[Mifv5]], [[The Dog NFT]], [[Projections]], and [[New Horses]] belong to his solo practice. The vault already has a longer artist note at [[Tojiba Brand Manager]] (a Grok draft, flagged needs-sources).
