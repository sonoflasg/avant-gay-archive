“Avant NFT” is the earlier name for the circle of artists and collectors that later writers fold into [[Avant Gay]]. Published accounts disagree about who coined it. This note sets those accounts side by side and lists the public posts that can still be checked. It does not pick a winner. Times are Melbourne time (AEDT).

### Accounts of the coinage, side by side

**The Automat paper (Giannis Sourdis, 9 Nov 2026).** The phrase “can be traced publicly to at least November 20, 2021, when [[Supermetal Bosch]] posted a meme describing ‘the 19 y/o avant NFT collector.’” The paper also cites a 16 Jan 2022 exchange in which [[Spiky DJ]] asked whether YOJIMBO_KING had coined the term and Charlotte Fang replied “it was bosch.” It treats these posts, with Spiky DJ’s later shout-out, as strong contemporaneous evidence that participants credited Bosch. It also records an anonymous early participant who questions whether inventing the phrase and building the social category are the same thing.

**GardenParty85, “[[Notes from the Avant NFT Underground]]” (Right Click Save, 3 Nov 2025).** The term “was a tongue-in-cheek comment first coined by the artist Super Metal Bosch in a Twitter post on January 25, 2022,” in a thread of memes that includes Bonkler, Milady Maker, Spiky DJ, J48BAFORMS, TOJI 100 and Bosch’s own projects. *Inference:* this matches Bosch’s “avant nft starter pack” post, which is 26 Jan 2022, 3:47pm AEDT, or 25 Jan in US time (https://x.com/supermetalx/status/1486199049111982082). That post is later than other uses listed below, so it may record when the term spread rather than its first use.

**[[lowbie]], “[[THERE IS NO HISTORY]]” (vvv.so, 23 Aug 2025), relaying [[Jared Madere]].** Who decided on “Avant” “is still up for debate – Jared Madere explained to me that supposedly it was coined by Spiky DJ or Milady/Charlotte Fang, Milady using it sincerely, and then Spiky laughing at it and running with it.” lowbie adds that the label hardened after an interview in which Madere said the circuit “jokingly refers to itself as ‘avant nft’,” and the interviewers cut “jokingly.”

**Artnet, “[[Wet Paint Avant-NFT movement]]” (Annie Armstrong, 11 Apr 2024).** It describes Galerie Yeche Lange’s specialty as “a movement that the artist [Madere] terms ‘avant NFT’.” This is the first mainstream press use found in this pass. It attributes the usage to Madere but makes no claim that he coined it.

### Public posts that can still be checked (earliest first)

### Reading the record

The live posts show the phrase in use within hours of the meme’s first known appearance. They also show that by mid-January 2022 Spiky DJ was unsure whether YOJIMBO_KING had coined it, and that ten days later he credited Bosch. *Inference:* YOJIMBO_KING’s 21 Nov post is a reaction to the meme, not an independent coinage. The record supports the Automat paper’s Bosch attribution better than it supports GardenParty85’s 25 Jan 2022 date. Neither of these settles the separate question, raised in lowbie’s and Madere’s account, of who turned a joke into the scene’s name.

### “Gay NFT”

The later name has one account across sources. GardenParty85 says it came from “a Twitter exchange between [[New Art Program]] and [[Tojiba Brand Manager]], who declared that calling things ‘avant’ has always been ‘pretty gay.’” lowbie says the scene “was coined ‘Gay’ in a series of tweets between Tojiba and New Art Program.” The Automat paper dates the exchange to April 2024. [[SonOfLasG]]’s essay [[Avant Gay]] quotes the same banter.

See also [[Remilia]], [[Supermetal Bosch]], [[Spiky DJ]], [[Avant Gay]].
