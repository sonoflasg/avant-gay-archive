[[111k]] is a large generative project organized by [[Parker Ito]] and [[Evil Biscuit]], with additional collaborators, that opened for minting on 11 November 2026 on Ethereum through [[vvv.so]] — VVV’s first Ethereum release. Intended total: 111,111 unique images. Official collection page: [vvv.so/111k](https://www.vvv.so/111k). This note is compiled by SonOfLasG from the artists’ public posts, from early critical readings, and from the [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]] (published two days before the mint), including the paper’s own caution that some attribution was still to be confirmed. It is a chapter on a project still unfolding, not a finished catalogue.

### How it extends, and departs from, the Solana-era scene

Most of the image-worlds folded into 111k were developed on [[Solana]] — [[Drifella]], [[Drilady]], [[Little Swag World]], [[Horses?7]], [[Pilgrim’s Living Room Crucifixion]], and others. 111k keeps that material in motion, but it does several things the earlier Solana collections did not do at once: it is VVV’s first Ethereum release; it is organized as many Collection Blocks inside one 111,111-work container; it is sold as nested crates, warehouse boxes, relics, and still-unhatched eggs rather than as a single flat mint; and the public window is a rolling reveal from 11/11 through 1/1, not a one-day drop.

Biscuit, introducing the Collection Block structure on 10 Nov 2026, 2:00am AEDT, wrote that making 111,111 “interesting and unique images” was hard to fathom, and that in this scene artists “tend to reinvent [their] art style or find a new format.” Each block, he said, either iterates on a style that had proliferated or “tapped back into a style of image making that we had used before,” sometimes stumbling onto new methods: https://x.com/bis__cut/status/2119807368980472012 and https://x.com/bis__cut/status/2119807377562067038

[[Giannis Sourdis]], in a 12 Nov 2026, 8:30pm AEDT post, argued that the work is not “another generative collection on ETH producing 111,111 outputs of the same thing,” but 27 named blocks by a group of artists, mixing painting, handmade and reworked assets, Photoshop collage, GANs, AI-assisted imagery, animation, and custom generative software, and building on “years of artistic dialogue” inside [[Avant Gay]]. He also recorded a live disagreement: some public criticism had called the project “AI slop,” boring, or extractive, and had treated Ethereum-scale supply as disqualifying. He treated those objections as real, while arguing that reducing the project to supply, mint price, or the use of AI “misses the actual artistic questions being explored”: https://x.com/Greekdx/status/2120811343913566386

That crossing from a Solana-native scene onto Ethereum, at this scale, with a shared trait pool and with blocks that remix one another’s characters, is the latest chapter in the scene’s evolution — still in progress, and still argued over in public.

### Mint, eggs, relics, and a rolling reveal

The mint is nested. Opening a container, according to the Automat paper, destroys the outer NFT. Ito’s public posts add that a relic “holds 111 crates,” and that a “god crate” is not simply bought. Eggs, Biscuit said, return a 2023 motif to the crates; the paper describes a fictional portal “intended to remain active until January 1, 2027.” Read next to the public 11/11–1/1 window, the “what will they hatch into?” posts, and “more Collection Blocks … til 1/1,” that is the public evidence for a rolling reveal rather than a single drop.

A metadata inventory of official token records for minted artwork IDs 1–27,960, compiled 14 Nov 2026 around 6:16am AEDT, counted 27 named Collection Blocks (19,604 labelled works), 585 minted works with no `collection block` trait — visually leftovers of already-named styles, i.e. a labelling gap, not a secret 28th block — and 7,770 still-unhatched eggs. Those counts will change as crates are opened and later blocks are added.

### Attributions, side by side, still in flux

Several accounts should be held at once, not merged.

**On who is in the project.** The Automat paper, drawing on a Le Random interview, lists contributors including Tojiba Brand Manager, Supermetal Bosch, meinong, Gremlins2Movie, Turtle, Dido Pynell, Cupid, cherub, luhhfella, and New Art Program, alongside programmer Ivan and the VVV team, and it states of at least one project image that “Exact attribution, medium, and production status [were] to be confirmed.” Biscuit’s 9 Nov tag list overlaps that roster and adds @123yoshistory, while not tagging Cupid or cherub. As of the 14 Nov snapshot, no live Collection Block title publicly matches Dido, New Art Program, or @123yoshistory, and none is publicly signed to Cupid or cherub. Those people may still appear in later reveal epochs, in unlabelled works, or in collaborations filed under another artist’s block. Dido and New Art Program have circulated 111k posts but have not, in the posts gathered here, named a block as theirs.

**On Parker’s blocks.** Biscuit said Ito “has made multiple sub blocks” of “vast scenes and large paintings,” one of them “depicting the realms and worlds that Drifella and Drilady come from,” and named [[Horses?7]] and [[Pilgrim’s Living Room Crucifixion]] as precedents. He did not give those blocks’ 111k titles. Ito’s own “Mind Body Spirit / tarot card system” post likewise names a system that does not match a live block title in the 14 Nov snapshot. This archive does not assign *plane of existence*, *a fella and a lady walk into a glue factory*, *hyperstacks*, or any other unsigned title to Ito on the strength of that description alone.

**On [[Drilady 2]].** Biscuit announced the block himself. The Automat paper, citing Ito, treats the 111k “drilady 2” sub-series as almost entirely a Biscuit-authored reinterpretation of Ito’s character — a reversal of the earlier Drifella-to-Drilady direction. Sourdis’s 12 Nov post states the same sequel reading in his own voice. Those public accounts agree on Biscuit as the 111k author of the sequel; they do not erase Ito’s authorship of the original [[Drilady]].

**On unsigned or only partly signed blocks.** Biscuit posted trait cards for Collage Paintings, Poets, Gamblers, Fools, and pixel chunk no captain chunk! without a “I made this block” line. Gremlins publicly grouped Poets with hyperstacks; that is an endorsement. *backrooms 2* closely matches Biscuit’s description of Gremlins’ liminal rooms, but the title is not itself a signature. *dratini fanart* is a high-confidence inference from meinong’s pre-mint post, not a signed credit line, and it is a different block from Turtle’s [[DriMi]] (which also starts from a Dratini outline). *watercolor mons* and *driflady* have strong author-side associations in Biscuit’s own pre-mint posts, not formal credit lines. *Puppet Readymade* and several other live titles remain unattributed in public.

Collaborator credits should still be treated as in flux where the paper itself says confirmation is pending, where Biscuit has promised more blocks and more featured artists before 1 January 2027, and where Matto’s public first response — “hard to know who made what” — is still an accurate description of parts of the metadata.

### Compared with the Automat paper’s updated 111k section

Automat announced on 18 Nov, 11:25pm AEDT, that it had “compiled all available public information around the recently minted out collaborative collection 111K”: https://x.com/Automatdotart/status/2123029939347697756. Read on 19 Nov, the paper’s 111k section ([[Automat Avant-Gay paper]]) and this note differ or add in these places. Both accounts are kept side by side:
- **Date of the hatch round.** Automat: “November 17, 2026.” This note: 18 Nov, 9:13am AEDT. Same posts; the difference is the time zone.
- **[[Collage Paintings]].** Automat calls it “a Bosch-led block incorporating assets from several participants,” with Dido contributing material, and says the relation of Biscuit’s 2,000 “hand-curated collage paintings” to that block “remains to be confirmed against the project’s metadata.” This note records Biscuit’s 12 Nov trait card for the block, his 18 Nov “my hand curated Collage Painting collection,” and epoch_gallery’s “collage painting by @bis__cut.” The community index shows one “Collage Paintings” name (2,362 indexed), not two. Unresolved.
- **Ito’s blocks.** Automat refers to “Ito’s landscape-format Realm environments.” Ito’s own 15 Nov reply says “Realm sub block”; he also named “John 7.” Neither is a block name in the index.
- **Scale of the first release.** Automat reports, from an X Space, that about 80,000 images were prepared for the first release and that the rest of the 111,111 is reserved for eggs. This archive has not heard that recording and records it as Automat’s account.
- **Containers.** Automat lists 505 warehouses of eleven crates and eighteen reliquaries of 111 crates. This note relies on Biscuit’s 9 Nov thread and Ito’s “holds 111 crates.” The figures agree.
- **[[DriFPS]] tools.** Automat lists the in-game tools (pistol through paintball gun) and a hundred simultaneous canvases, citing New Art Program’s 18 Nov statement. The visible part of that post, quoted in [[DriFPS]], does not show the list; this note leaves it attributed to Automat.
- **Sell-out.** Automat’s announcement says “recently minted out” without a date. This note dates it from VVV’s posts.
