[[Apartment Traveler]] is a Collection Block inside [[111k]] by [[Tojiba Brand Manager]] (X: @m_m_____m____mm). [[Evil Biscuit]], on 10 Nov 2026, 2:00am AEDT, credited “Toji Brand Manager” with “a plethora of sub blocks,” including “a 3d modeled chinese apartment with first person horror video game aesthetics.”

On 13 Nov 2026, 2:41pm AEDT, Tojiba posted an information thread in his own voice. He describes a base of about 215 images taken from Chinese real-estate websites in less populated provinces; a fiction of players trapped in a labyrinth of apartment listings whose rooms warp as they are revisited, not as one player’s continuous playthrough but as an entire player base moving through an interconnected system of rooms; and an invitation from Biscuit to iterate on images Tojiba had made in 2018. Later posts in the same thread note a hands/arms asset class with its own rarity; about 470 works rendered in Blender, with the 2D apartments built “very plainly” into 3D spaces; assets from the shared 111k trait pool and odd-shaped portals; and [[Drifriends]] characters (credited there to [[meinong]]) crossing into the 2D apartments. A 14 Nov morning metadata snapshot counted 1,453 labelled works — far more than the 215 base images, consistent with the thread’s account of iterated rooms.
