[[Evil Biscuit]], June 2026. Solana / mons.shop. 11,133 cards. Digital and physical trading-card system.

Registered in Appendix B of the Automat paper as part of the wider [[Avant Gay]] ecosystem (Cards, Objects, Games, and Physical-Digital Systems).

Compiled by SonOfLasG from that paper’s public project register; this is a stub, not a full entry.

### Added 14/11/2026

- **Debut.** Le Random’s June 2026 interview introduces card nft 2 as the trading-card project “first appearing with SOLOS Gallery at Felix Art Fair in February 2026.” See [[SOLOS]].
- **Size, side by side.** Before release, Biscuit told Le Random there would be “around 11,000 cards,” minted in “more than 3,000 packs.” The Automat paper registers 11,133 cards. Giannis Sourdis’s Right Click Save forum post (16 Jun 2026, AEST) describes 3,000 packs of 3 card NFTs on mons.shop, which would be about 9,000 cards. The sources have not been reconciled.
- **Precursor.** Biscuit names [[Poncho Drifella]] (“Poncho Driff,” 200 cards) as the precursor: each card could be redeemed for the physical object.
- **Physical base.** Half the set was scanned at high DPI from pre-existing trading cards with collage on top, so they could be printed on redemption. The other half relate to holographic physical cards. Some cards were hand-painted in watercolour and rescanned as an altered rarity.
- **Access.** Stripe card payments were added on mons.shop, so people without crypto could buy. [[meinong]] built a binder viewer for the first card nft series, hosted at cards.art.
