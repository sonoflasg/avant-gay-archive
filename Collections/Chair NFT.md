A 2023 Ethereum collection associated with the [[Cuckcore]] / Fella milieu, often discussed alongside [[Cuckfella]] and [[Chucks]]. In the scene’s lore it is closely tied to the [[chair]] punchline — the empty chair as an endpoint after narrative excess.

[[Giannis Sourdis]] documents the project in the [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]] (9 Nov 2026). [[Brian Droitcour]], in a written interview for that paper, singles out Chair NFT, [[Cuckfella]], and [[Chucks]] as works that lean hard into the language of the cucking fetish, which he suggests may have affected how often they are later discussed.
