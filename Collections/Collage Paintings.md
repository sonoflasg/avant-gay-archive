[[Collage Paintings]] is a Collection Block inside [[111k]]. Public accounts of who made it differ, and they are kept side by side here.

**Biscuit’s posts.** On 12 Nov 2026, 8:36am AEDT, [[Evil Biscuit]] posted an open trait card for Collage Paintings (Rauschenberg switch, underpaintings, sprites), without an “I made this block” line. **Automat’s account.** The [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]]’s updated 111k section calls Collage Paintings “a Bosch-led block incorporating assets from several participants,” with [[Dido Pynell]] contributing material, which points to [[Supermetal Bosch]]. It says the relation of Biscuit’s 2,000 hand-curated works to that block “remains to be confirmed against the project’s metadata.”

**Collector attribution.** On 19 Nov 2026, 8:17am AEDT, epoch_gallery wrote that it had “picked up another collage painting by @bis__cut from 111k.”

*Inference, unconfirmed*: the community index at 111k.page (generated 18 Nov 2026, 8:18pm AEDT) shows a single “Collage Paintings” name, with 2,362 revealed tokens, and no second collage-painting block. That suggests Biscuit’s 2,000 are filed under the same block title, but it does not settle authorship. Biscuit has said artist credits will be added to the block JSONs, and those credits should decide it.
