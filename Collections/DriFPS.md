[[DriFPS]] is a Collection Block inside [[111k]] by [[New Art Program]] (X: @newartprogram). It entered the collection in the egg-hatch round of 18 Nov 2026.

[[Evil Biscuit]], 18 Nov 2026, 9:13am AEDT: “1500 supply of @newartprogram collection block titled DriFPS created with a first person shooter he vibecoded to spray assets and paint across canvases with multiple different guns and ammunition types.” Part of that supply was split with hatching eggs. Biscuit wrote that the rest “will now have the possibility to be in crates.”

New Art Program, quote-posting it the same day at 2:52pm AEDT: “for my contribution to 111k i wanted to give a new art program spin on the drifella world. me and @duncanswilson talk about digital painting mouse mechanics having overlap with fps games. so the next step is to make that real. i wanted to shoot the drif trait trove at canvases.” The visible text of the post ends there.

The [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]]’s updated 111k section, citing the same statement, lists the in-game tools (pistol, submachine gun, assault rifle, shotgun, rocket launcher, paintbrush, and paintball gun) and says the program could target a hundred canvases at once. That detail is recorded here as Automat’s account; the visible part of the post does not show it.

The community index at 111k.page (generated 18 Nov 2026, 8:18pm AEDT) listed 74 revealed DriFPS tokens, out of the 1,500 supply Biscuit announced.

Earlier, the Le Random interview had New Art Program “making some paintings” for 111k, without a block title. See [[111k]].
