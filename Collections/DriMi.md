[[DriMi]] is a Collection Block inside [[111k]] by [[Turtle]] (X: @turtletimezone). On 10 Nov 2026, 2:01am AEDT, [[Evil Biscuit]] wrote that Turtle “reached back into the aesthetic of the simple pfp and derivatives of [[Milady]] Maker and @Dri_Fella starting with the base outline of a [[Dratini]],” with hats, shirts, and accessories swapping to make new character designs. On 12 Nov 2026, 8:30pm AEDT, [[Giannis Sourdis]] described DriMi as taking the [[Drifella]] character in another direction, “playing with and satirizing the conventions of rugcore and the previous cycle’s PFP boom.” A 14 Nov morning metadata snapshot counted 590 labelled works.

This is a different block from [[dratini fanart]].
