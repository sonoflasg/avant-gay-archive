*Drifella III* is a collection by [[Evil Biscuit]], released on 4 September 2025 on [[Solana]] through [[vvv.so]] (Automat register). It is the third main series after [[Drifella]] and [[Drifella 2]].

### Structure

Peter Bauman (Le Random, 9 Oct 2025) describes it as “a series of 1,333 rooms containing 3,999 chaos-layered, reference-maxxed paintings.” “Each room is a triptych containing up to 78 different traits,” and the collection “has over 5,000 traits.” For comparison, he notes that CryptoPunks uses 87 traits across its whole 10,000. He recommends viewing it on d347h.eth’s custom frontend, [[Drifellascape]], rather than in a feed. The Automat paper gives the same 1,333 rooms and 3,999 paintings.

### Origins, as told in the sources

- **The Haircuts work.** Bauman cites [[lowbie]]: “Without crucifella, there is no Drifella III,” because the large physical crucidrifella in Yeche Lange’s show [[Haircuts]] (2024) “helped inspire” the series’ painterly turn. The Automat paper says the same, citing Biscuit.
- **MiFella lore.** Bauman gives scene lore told to him by [[Patrician]]. MiFella struggled to mint out on Solana, and the community joked that it would mint out only once the artist died. That joke led to the crucifella figure, which first appeared in [[Constant Fella|The Constant Fella]].
- **Chaos layering.** Bauman names [[MiFella 2]] and [[Cuckfella]] as inspirations.
- **Scene references.** Biscuit described Drifella in a March 2023 tweet as “a punk mash-up of milady. Mifella and Dratini” (quoted by Bauman). Bauman also notes that the lore is “getting crystallized now by the artist with writers like SonofLasG.”

### Physical work and reception

- **Physical redemption.** The Automat paper says this was VVV’s first physical-redemption release, extending the collection into framed embellished watercolour prints, larger works, an Alien Triptych redemption and a Rare Evil Twin mechanic. Biscuit began 3D-printed frames and silicone molds while preparing [[One of Us Is Real and It's Not You]].
- **Le Random timeline.** “If 2025 marked wider acceptance of the avant NFT scene, its culminating statement was Drifella III,” Evil Biscuit’s “hyper-‘schizocollage’ project.” Le Random’s “Standouts of 2025” ranks it 16th.
- **Jared Madere.** Bauman reports that Madere called Drifella 2 “the most interesting conversation in painting in 30 years.”

See also [[Schizocollage]], [[trait-maxxing]], [[Le Random]].

### Critical readings (added 17/11/2026)

- **Brian Droitcour, Outland, 23 Dec 2025** ([[After the Art World]]): "Each work shows a triptych installed in a room." He reads the collection "as an image of categories dissolving. Collectibles melt into art; art melts into collectibles."
- **Outland, "Context Creator," 12 Nov 2025:** "Biscuit's Drifella III uses the collage generator's depth of field to mimic exhibition views, where his past works appear as paintings hanging on a wall, while figurines stand below them on the floor."
