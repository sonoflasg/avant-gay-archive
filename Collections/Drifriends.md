[[Drifriends]] is a Collection Block inside [[111k]] by [[meinong]] (X: @urmeinong). On 10 Nov 2026, 2:00am AEDT, [[Evil Biscuit]] wrote that meinong “created a friend slop fivercore 3d asset system and generated unique variations of Drifellas and Driladys,” and that the figures would also appear in a “multiplayer metaverse style Collider Launch party.” A 14 Nov morning metadata snapshot counted 1,033 labelled works.

On 13 Nov 2026, [[Tojiba Brand Manager]], in the [[Apartment Traveler]] thread, wrote that Drifriends characters sometimes cross over into those 2D apartments, as player characters sometimes cross into their 3D ones, and credited the characters to meinong: https://x.com/m_m_____m____mm/status/2121086054132220034

This is a different block from [[dratini fanart]], which is associated with meinong by inference rather than by this kind of signed credit line, and from [[DriMi]], which is credited to [[Turtle]].
