[[Drilady 2]] is a Collection Block inside [[111k]], announced by [[Evil Biscuit]] on 7 Nov 2026, 5:41am AEDT, as “a collection block inside 111k,” dated 11/11. A 12 Nov, 8:35am AEDT trait card labels the work type as painting and includes a “lola bunny squad” body field among other traits. A 14 Nov morning metadata snapshot counted 1,244 labelled works.

The original [[Drilady]] (2025, [[Solana]]) is by [[Parker Ito]]. On 12 Nov 2026, 8:30pm AEDT, [[Giannis Sourdis]] described the 111k block as Biscuit “creating the sequel of Drilady … with a new silhouette and artistic direction.” The Automat paper, citing Ito, likewise treats the sub-series as almost entirely a Biscuit-authored reinterpretation of Ito’s character. Those public accounts agree on the 111k authorship; they do not replace Ito’s authorship of the earlier collection.
