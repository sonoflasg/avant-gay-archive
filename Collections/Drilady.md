[[Drilady]] is a 2025 [[Solana]] collection by [[Parker Ito]]. This file was an empty stub in the vault; neighboring notes already assumed it. The vault’s [[Horses?2]], [[trait-maxxing]], and [[Schizocollage]] drafts (all Grok-authored, flagged needs-sources) name Drilady as one of Ito’s key on-chain works. Public circulation on X treated it as a flagship of the scene — for example a 4 Oct 2025 post Ito reposted, “Drilady will be one of the most sought after Avant Gay NFT collections”: https://x.com/CreamyDreamy/status/1974372937378697584 (6:16pm AEDT). This entry is compiled by SonOfLasG from those pointers and from the public account in the [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]] (case study 4.6). It is not Ito’s own catalogue text.

### What the paper records

According to that paper’s interview with Ito (August 2026), Drilady was designed to keep a recognizable conventional attractiveness while traits crowded the figure. Ito frames the character as a transformation of [[Drifella]] back toward “lady” and into a bunny, with Lola Bunny as a template that could hold glamour and grotesquerie together. Fashion traits — including specific archival garments Ito says he already knew from years of buying vintage clothing — are described as part of that construction, not as decoration.

On process, the paper records both inheritance and remaking. [[Evil Biscuit]] sent Ito a folder while explaining trait weighting; it contained only part of the [[Drifella 2]] archive, which Ito says he did not realize at the time. He printed and painted over selected elements, scanned them back, and built clothing anew because Drilady’s body geometry did not fit Drifella’s. The paper’s formulation is that the collection “remains derivative in genealogy, spirit, and concept even as many individual assets depart substantially from their sources.”

Two scarce configurations are named there: Full Metal Bunny (30 works) and Beautiful Geisha (50; metadata “Asian GF” when active). On 18 Oct 2025 Ito released [[The Ecstasy of Saint Drilady]] on Exchange Art after a nun asset made for Drilady failed to appear in the generated set; the paper describes that release as an accidental afterlife of an unused asset, not a planned expansion.

### Accounts that differ

**On audience.** One early participant cited in the paper saw Drilady as bringing the Drifella lineage to a different audience. Ito himself, in the same study, is more cautious and points to a wider rise in attention around his Verse project and [[Drifella III]]. The paper declines to treat Drilady as the sole cause of that shift.

**On later reuse.** Inside [[111k]] (mint opened 11 Nov 2026), Biscuit released a “drilady 2” Collection Block, announced publicly on 7 Nov 2026, 5:41am AEDT: https://x.com/bis__cut/status/2118775893250224345. The paper, citing Ito, treats that sub-series as almost entirely a Biscuit-authored reinterpretation of Ito’s character — a reversal of the earlier Drifella-to-Drilady direction.
