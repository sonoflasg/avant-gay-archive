[[Spiky DJ]] , July 2022. Ethereum. 376 works.

Registered in Appendix B of the Automat paper as part of the wider [[Avant Gay]] ecosystem (Antecedents and Early Avant NFT).

Compiled by SonOfLasG from that paper’s public project register; this is a stub, not a full entry.

GardenParty85 ([[Notes from the Avant NFT Underground]]) describes Spiky DJ as “a project by the collective, FPBJPC.” Its long-standing tag line is “never minting out,” and spiky headphone traits recur in [[MiFella]] (dated 2022 in that essay), [[Drifella]] (2023) and [[Little Swag World]] (2024). Spiky DJ work “can be found in galleries, public restrooms in Berlin, and on the blockchain.” Peter Bauman (Le Random, 9 Oct 2025) calls Spiky DJ the “creator of Mifella”; other accounts differ (see [[MiFella]]). The account’s posts are central to the [[Avant NFT]] naming record.
