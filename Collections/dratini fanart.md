[[dratini fanart]] is a Collection Block inside [[111k]]. It does not carry a signed “this is my Collection Block” line in the posts gathered here. On 31 Oct 2026, 6:29am AEDT, [[meinong]] (@urmeinong) posted “dratini fan arts” with a set of images; [[Evil Biscuit]] and [[Gremlins2Movie]] later reposted that post. The official block title is essentially the title of that pre-mint post, and official tokens have been matched to images from it. This archive therefore records a high-confidence inference that meinong made or curated the block, and marks it as an inference, not a formal credit.

The pictures use [[Dratini]] / [[Pokémon]] fan-art source material as collage. Do not confuse this block with [[DriMi]] by [[Turtle]], which Biscuit separately credited as starting from “the base outline of a dratini” in a PFP / accessory-swap format.
