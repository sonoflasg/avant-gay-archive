[[little swag]] is a Collection Block inside [[111k]] by [[Supermetal Bosch]] (X: @supermetalx). On 3 Nov 2026, 5:00am AEDT, Bosch posted “drifella and drilady swag for 111k,” which [[Parker Ito]] reposted the same morning. On 10 Nov 2026, 2:00am AEDT, [[Evil Biscuit]] wrote that Bosch “has made these little swag worlds that have been polymerized together with a drifella,” “taking style transfer and gpt image gen to the next level.” A 14 Nov morning metadata snapshot counted 331 labelled works, in two sub-blocks: `little swag drif` (238) and `cruciswag` (93). The block is related to, and distinct from, Bosch’s earlier [[Little Swag World]] on [[Solana]].
