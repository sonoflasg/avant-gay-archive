Drifellascape is a custom web frontend by d347h.eth for viewing [[Drifella III]]. Peter Bauman argues that because the collection is made of triptychs in rooms, “viewing the collection in any feed is unsuitable. To experience the collection as intended, it should be viewed on d347h.eth’s custom frontend, Drifellascape.”
