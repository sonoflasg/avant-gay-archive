*Haircuts* was the first exhibition at [[Galerie Yeche Lange]]’s physical New York space at 11 Broadway. It ran from 17 May to 14 July 2024.

The seven artists were Earl ([[Earl @earl16o1]]), [[Wretched Worm]], Valerie Keane, [[Jared Madere]], [[Evil Biscuit]], Yasmin Kaytmaz and Pauly Stone Jiva. The Automat paper and Le Random give the same list. The paper reads the line-up as Madere’s aim of placing artists from his contemporary-art circle beside crypto-native artists.

Biscuit made the large physical work *crucidrifella with bloody margielas* for the show, with about five to eight physical Drifella cards fixed to its surface. He credits making it with pushing [[Drifella III]] in a more painterly direction. Le Random’s Drifella III essay cites [[lowbie]] for the same link: the crucidrifella at Yeche’s *Haircuts* inspired Drifella III.

Artnet’s 11 Apr 2024 column had announced that the space would open “next month at 11 Broadway”; see [[Wet Paint Avant-NFT movement]].
