*One Of Us Is Real And It’s Not You* was a two-person exhibition of [[Supermetal Bosch]] and [[Evil Biscuit]] at [[Galerie Yeche Lange]], 11 Broadway, New York. The Automat paper gives the opening as 26 June 2025. Droitcour calls it a summer 2025 “two-man show.” Le Random’s timeline calls it “a group show.”

**What was shown.** Bosch showed Mons ceramics, figures, obelisks and playable chess-like game boards. Biscuit showed [[Drifella]] works, including framed paintings, a large triptych and cast metal cards (Automat; Le Random timeline). The Automat paper says that while preparing for the show Biscuit began 3D-printing frames and making silicone molds, and then folded physical versions into [[Drifella III]].

**Readings.**
- Peter Bauman (Le Random timeline) called it “one of 2025’s most impactful shows,” an “‘avant NFT’ showcase of millennial nostalgia.” He wrote that it signified, with the gallery’s 2024 opening, “the wider acceptance of the avant NFT scene, after years of development and a period of self-exile from Ethereum to Solana.”
- Brian Droitcour ([[Seams and Synthesis]]) used the show to frame the tension between visible seams and AI synthesis. It placed Bosch’s Mons and [[Little Swag World]] beside Biscuit’s Drifella.
- The Automat paper treats it as the clearest case of Yeche bringing crypto-native work into a gallery setting.

Le Random’s timeline lists the gallery team at the time as curators Jared Madere, Wretched Worm and Supermetal Bosch, editor Anastasios Karnazes and developer Miles Peyton. Other sources name the team differently; see [[Galerie Yeche Lange]].
