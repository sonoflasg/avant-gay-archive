Milo Conroy is named by GardenParty85 as one of the curators of the first, online-only [[Galerie Yeche Lange]], launched in 2022. The other curators were [[Jared Madere]] and Wretched Wurm ([[Wretched Worm]]), with editor Anastasios Karnazes and developer [[Miles Peyton]].

This rests on one source. Later sources do not include Conroy:
- Le Random’s 2025 timeline lists the curators as Madere, Wretched Worm and [[Supermetal Bosch]].
- Le Random’s Aug 2026 interview bio says Madere “co-founded Galerie Yeche Lange with Wretched Worm and Miles Peyton.”
- The Automat paper does not mention Conroy.
GardenParty85 also says the gallery’s “format and personnel changed” in spring 2024, which may account for the difference (inference). An earlier Grok draft in this vault ([[Jared Madere]]) also listed Conroy among Yeche’s co-founders, without a source.
