[[Brian Droitcour]] was interviewed by Giannis Sourdis for the Automat paper (written interview, September 2026).

The paper’s public References section records that this interview was used for: How he came to Gay NFT / [[Avant Gay]]; its relation to [[post-internet art]]; how he reads [[trait-maxxing]] alongside [[Schizocollage]]; PFP culture and split online identity; critical responses to [[Cuckcore]] works including [[Cuckfella]], [[Chucks]], and [[Chair NFT]]; and whether “Avant/Gay” is better treated as scene slang or as an art-historical term.

This note records only what is published there. It is not a transcript, and it does not add private correspondence.

Compiled by SonOfLasG from the paper’s References.

Published writing in this archive: [[Seams and Synthesis]] (Le Random, 2 Feb 2026) and [[Two Trends in NFT Art]] (biānjiè.systems, 9 May 2026). Neither claims that he coined “schizocollage”; see [[Schizocollage]].
