Edmund (X: @EdmundGettier, display name Edmund) is a commentator in the [[Avant Gay]] scene. From at least August 2026 he has used public replies to point readers to collections and artists, often naming [[MiFella]], [[Little Swag World]], [[Drifella]] and [[Drifella III]], [[Tojiba]], [[Drilady]], [[Evil Biscuit]], [[Parker Ito]] and [[Supermetal Bosch]].
