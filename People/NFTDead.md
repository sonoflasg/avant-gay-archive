NFTDead (X: @NFTD3AD) was interviewed by Giannis Sourdis for the Automat paper (written interview, September 2026). The public X display name, as of 19 Nov 2026, is “NFTDead 🥱🙏”. Previously filed as NFTDEAD; also searchable as nftdad.

The paper’s public References section records that this interview was used for: Coming to [[Avant Gay]] as a collector through [[MiFella]] and [[Little Swag World]]; Solana’s low-friction circulation; the earlier terms Avant NFT and Gay NFT; and why they archive group chats, deleted posts, and dead links so later viewers can make up their own minds.

This note records only what is published there. It is not a transcript, and it does not add private correspondence.

### Public posts

On X, NFTDead (@NFTD3AD) posts often as a collector and commentator in the [[Avant Gay]] scene. In mid-November 2026 they wrote about the [[111k]] mint, about earlier work on Solana, and about [[MiFella]] and [[Planet Peppa]].

- **On Solana and 111k (14 Nov 2026, 7:51pm AEDT):** “Avant/gay on Solana was always about experimentation, iteration and the occasional breakthrough. Much of the work was repetition and rumination that added little to the conversation. But every so often, something genuinely exciting emerged and pushed the scene forward.” The post is tagged 111k: https://x.com/NFTD3AD/status/2121526393347461511
- **On minting 111k (15 Nov 2026, 4:09am AEDT):** they wrote that a few hours was “the difference between you being the permachad that minted 111k or the pathetic chud that faded the most exciting mint on ethereum since 2021 and had to go crawling to secondary once everyone else told you how good it was.” https://x.com/NFTD3AD/status/2121651812650438676
- **On LaunchMyNFT (16 Nov 2026, 8:03pm AEDT):** “Avant/gay: powered by @LaunchMyNFT”: https://x.com/NFTD3AD/status/2122254204869959738 See [[LaunchMyNFT]].
- **On Planet Peppa (6 Nov 2026, 5:17pm AEDT):** “planet peppa looks good here”: https://x.com/NFTD3AD/status/2118588470226833829 See [[Planet Peppa]].
