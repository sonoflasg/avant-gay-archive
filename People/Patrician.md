[[Patrician]] (X: @SAlgotrader, display name 「Patrician」, https://x.com/SAlgotrader) is an artist in the MiFella, Cuckcore and Fella networks. His X bio reads “PiFella daddy.” The Automat paper credits him with [[PiFellas]] (March 2024), [[PiFella 2]] (*PiFella 2 Parasite Chamber*, with Eamz, September 2025) and [[Yeche Lunch]] (with eamz and hungry bug, 2024).

### Interview in the Automat paper

[[Patrician]] was interviewed by Giannis Sourdis for the Automat paper (interview, 30 Jul 2026). The paper’s public References section records that this interview was used for: Early [[MiFella]], [[Cuckcore]], and Fella networks; [[LaunchMyNFT]]’s role in widening participation in 2024; token communities; and how market activity, social organization, and image-making were bound together, including projects such as [[PiFellas]].

This note records only what is published there. It is not a transcript, and it does not add private correspondence.

Elsewhere the paper records that:
- Patrician’s *Everything Is PFP* was made for the October 2025 MiFella show in Athens, organised by Victor Lizana.
- Kaiotei describes him as an early “archangel” and “a kind of a founding member” of [[13-33 - 1333 Covenant|13<33]].
- He encouraged hungry bug and guppy to collaborate, and pointed the collector Varrock to *PiFella 2 Parasite Chamber*.
- The paper also cites an X post in which he invites readers to “read this whole account as part of the art.”
