[[Peter Bauman]] was interviewed by Giannis Sourdis for the Automat paper (interview, 4 Sep 2026).

The paper’s public References section records that this interview was used for: How he encountered [[Avant Gay]] and how it has been written about; [[trait-maxxing]] in relation to generative-art history; affinities with Pop, Post-Internet art, and Victorian photocollage; [[vvv.so]] compared with other generative platforms; and a caution against flattening the scene’s history too early.

This note records only what is published there. It is not a transcript, and it does not add private correspondence.

Compiled by SonOfLasG from the paper’s References.

Peter Bauman (Monk Antony) is editor in chief of [[Le Random]]. His Le Random essays and interviews are cited in [[Drifella III]], [[Heavy Liquid Graphic]], [[Card NFT 2]], [[vvv.so]], [[111k]] and [[One of Us Is Real and It's Not You]].
