### In the Automat paper

The [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]] (Giannis Sourdis, 9 Nov 2026) cites several of his writings in its References:
- "AGI. On the Term 'Avant Gay'", used for the origin and meaning of the term.
- "Iridium Pilled: Notes on the Avant/Gay".
- A Drifella series (The Legend of Drifella onward).
- A MiFella series beginning with "All Roads Lead to MiFella: A Primer".
- A Jim Spindle series.
- "The Image After Too Much Contact".
- A trait-genealogy diagram "tracing recurring motifs across MiFella, Drifella, Drifella 2, Little Swag World, and Drilady" ([[MiFella]], [[Drifella]], [[Drifella 2]], [[Little Swag World]], [[Drilady]]). One figure in the paper is marked "Adapted from Sonoflasg".

The paper's acknowledgements thank "SonOfLasG, Maxand98, and Varrock for the writing, documentation, archival resources, ideas, and perspectives that informed the study". SonOfLasG is not among the 44 people interviewed for it.

### Other mentions

Peter Bauman, writing on [[Drifella III]] for Le Random (9 Oct 2025), notes that the scene's lore is "getting crystallized now by the artist with writers like SonofLasG". See [[Peter Bauman]] and [[Le Random]].

### This archive

On 4 May 2026, 9:38am AEST, he posted a short video of a node map. The post says: "I've been mapping the connective tissue behind it... Each node in the video a thread, reference, recurring image, shared language". It asks readers to drop "posts, essays, fragments" in the replies: https://x.com/Sonoflasg/status/2051083913343521094

The repository README says the archive "started as one person's Obsidian vault". The earliest dated drafts in the vault are from 2–3 May 2026. *Inference:* the 4 May post shows that vault in progress.

The public repository (first commit 5 Jul 2026) and site are https://github.com/sonoflasg/avant-gay-archive and https://avantgay.sonoflasg.com/. He is the repository's maintainer. Contributions follow CONTRIBUTING.md and AGENTS.md.
