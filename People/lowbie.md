[[lowbie]] was interviewed by Giannis Sourdis for the Automat paper (interview, 2026).

The paper’s public References section records that this interview was used for: [[Avant Gay]] history and terminology; the genealogy around [[MiFella]], [[Drifella]], and related collections; [[Cigawrette Packs]], POSTCARDs, and community archiving; and a later essay on [[New Bad Image]] arguing that the pictures alone could not reproduce the social world that had drawn him to the scene.
