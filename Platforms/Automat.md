[[Automat]] is a digital-art initiative that presents itself as focused on collecting, researching, and presenting work shaped by the digital era. Its public research lab published the [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]] by co-founder and lead curator [[Giannis Sourdis]] in November 2026, alongside shorter essays on computer-art and generative figures. Related “living” artist pages include the [[Evil Biscuit Automat profile]] and the [[Parker Ito Automat profile]].
