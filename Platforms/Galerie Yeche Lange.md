Galerie Yeche Lange (also Yeche Lange; website yl.baby) is a gallery and platform for crypto-native art. It ran online first, then from 2024 to 2026 had a physical space at 11 Broadway in New York’s Financial District. [[vvv.so]] grew out of it. Sources agree on its importance but differ on its start date and its team.

### Online beginnings

- **GardenParty85** ([[Notes from the Avant NFT Underground]]): “The initial, online-only iteration of the gallery launched in 2022.” The artworks it sold were minted on Ethereum, explored through a web-based viewing environment. It reproduces an installation view of “School of Truth” at Yeche Lange, 2023.
- **Artnet, 11 Apr 2024** ([[Wet Paint Avant-NFT movement]]): Madere had been “operating Galerie Yeche Lange online” “for the past three years.” At that point it “currently exists only on Discord.” *Inference:* an online start around 2021.
- **The Automat paper**: “Online from 2022.” It also says Yeche tested customised mint interfaces and physical redemption, for example Jim Spindle’s *Scarecrow*, before VVV launched.
- **Madere** (Le Random, ep. 28, 10 Oct 2025) describes early Yeche mints as hyper-bespoke: 3D mint environments and auction landscapes such as the “Yeychelles,” a name he credits to [[New Art Program]].

### Who ran it, side by side

- GardenParty85, Nov 2025, describing the online gallery from 2022: curators [[Milo Conroy]], [[Jared Madere]] and Wretched Wurm; editor Anastasios Karnazes; developer [[Miles Peyton]]. Personnel “changed” in spring 2024.
- Artnet, Apr 2024: “cofounded by” Jared Madere.
- Le Random timeline, 2025 entry: curators Jared Madere, [[Wretched Worm]] and [[Supermetal Bosch]]; editor Anastasios Karnazes; developer Miles Peyton.
- Le Random, Aug 2026 bio: Madere “co-founded Galerie Yeche Lange with Wretched Worm and Miles Peyton.”

### New York, 2024–2026

- **Soft launch.** Early April 2024, at Dean Kissick’s gallery Earth, with a Super Metal Mons tournament (Artnet).
- **[[Haircuts]].** 17 May–14 Jul 2024, the first show at 11 Broadway. The artists were Earl, Wretched Worm, Valerie Keane, Jared Madere, Evil Biscuit, Yasmin Kaytmaz and Pauly Stone Jiva.
- **Chains.** Madere says that once the New York space opened, “we transitioned from ETH to Solana when Super Metal Bosch put out [[Little Swag World]]” (Le Random, Aug 2026).
- **[[One of Us Is Real and It's Not You]].** Supermetal Bosch and Evil Biscuit, opened 26 Jun 2025.
- **Fella Royale.** Opened 21 Feb 2026, with Milton Magoo, Evil Biscuit, [[luhhfella]], [[MiFella]] and Maidin. The Automat paper calls it the final exhibition at 11 Broadway, on view through the end of March 2026.

Other New York shows recorded by the Automat paper include *Wendy* (2024–25) and *Upside-down Promises* (2025).

### Yeche and VVV

[[vvv.so]] developed from Yeche. GardenParty85 says “Yeche built” it in early 2025, and Le Random’s timeline calls VVV “the digital arm of Galerie Yeche Lange.” VVV’s co-founders Madere and fairybaby say the idea grew when Madere brought in Wretched Worm and Miles Peyton, “who he had been running Yeche Lange with.” See that entry.

### More press accounts, side by side (added 17/11/2026)

- **According to Bankless (Metaversal), 15/02/2024:** Yeche Lange was "born in June 2022" and "comes from the minds of curators Jared Madere, Milo Conroy, Wretched Worm, and developer Miles Peyton." It names the inaugural show "Tournament for Swollen Hearts Round Un." This date sits beside Artnet's "for the past three years" (about 2021) and GardenParty85's 2022. Source: [[The Rise of Avant-Garde NFTs]]
- **According to Archetype, 14/05/2024:** the team "also includes Milo Conroy, Wretched Worm, Miles Peyton, Supermetal Bosch, Anastasios Karnazes, and Hikkimourning." Madere says the 2022 Pie Keys collection "basically funded the gallery treasury for two years." Source: [[In Conversation With Jared Madere (Archetype)]]
- **According to Andrew Russeth, Artnet News, 21/12/2025:** Yeche "has been doing outrageous shows in an office at the tip of Manhattan (though it's suddenly gone dark)." This is a single-source report; no other source here confirms or explains it.
- Reviews of [[One of Us Is Real and It's Not You]]: see [[Press coverage]].
