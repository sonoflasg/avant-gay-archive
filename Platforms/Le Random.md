Le Random is an online publication about generative and digital art, with editorials, a podcast with transcripts, and the Generative Art Timeline. [[Peter Bauman]] (Monk Antony) is its editor in chief. Since 2025 it has covered the [[Avant Gay]] scene more closely than any other outlet.

**Its definition.** Le Random’s “avant/gay” subject page defines the term as the name for the Solana avant scene around [[vvv.so|VVV]], which it also calls the “Remilia-Solana-VVV scene.” The Automat paper warns that narratives focused mainly on VVV can overlook earlier infrastructure such as [[LaunchMyNFT]]. Kissick’s [[The Vulgar Image]] puts MiFella’s Cuckcore at the centre instead. These framings sit side by side; see [[Avant NFT]].
