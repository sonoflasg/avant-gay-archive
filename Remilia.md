[[Remilia]] (Remilia Corporation / Remilia Collective) is the Ethereum-era net-art group around [[Milady]] Maker, launched 25 August 2021. This archive already points at that collection from several angles: [[SonOfLasG]]’s primer [[All Roads Lead to MiFella]] (15 Sep 2025, https://x.com/Sonoflasg/status/1967509090550394906) starts “at the fracture,” reading [[MiFella]] as the place where Milady’s DNA “mutated into something darker and messier”; the vault’s [[Milady]] note (a Grok draft, flagged needs-sources) already lists miladymaker.net and the Remilia wiki as links. This entry does not replace those notes. It records, in one place, the public disagreements about authorship and aftermath that later writing about [[Avant Gay]] keeps running into.

### Public outline

The [[Automat Avant-Gay paper]] (Giannis Sourdis, 9 Nov 2026) treats Remilia as a milieu rather than a single project. It dates the collective’s public arrival to *I Long For Network Spirituality*, an exhibition at Hunters & Collectors in New Zealand on 11 June 2021, and describes [[Charlotte Fang]] as the group’s principal public, theoretical, and organizational voice — while warning that this prominence should not be conflated with the visual authorship of [[Milady]].

### Accounts that differ

**On who made Milady’s images.** The paper reports Remilia’s later official account of the collection as a broad collaboration (assets, illustration, graphics, composition, development, promotion). It also reports that several people interviewed for the study, independently, identify [[Henry Sprite]] as the principal visual artist behind the underlying drawings, with Fang’s contribution described as conceptual, theoretical, organizational, and performative. The paper’s own formulation is that the evidence supports a collaborative model “while leaving the relative weight of individual contributions disputed.” This archive follows that split: both accounts are recorded; neither is selected as the truth of the page.

**On “Avant NFT.”** Contemporaneous public posts show the phrase circulating in late 2021 and early 2022. [[Spiky DJ]] wrote on 30 Dec 2021, “Going to start rocking with the term “Avant-NFT””: https://x.com/spiky_dj_/status/1476343546449956873 (11:05am AEDT). On 27 Jan 2022, 7:16am AEDT, the same account posted a shout-out to [[Supermetal Bosch]] “for coining the term avant NFT”: https://x.com/spiky_dj_/status/1486432931811889156, which Bosch reposted. Bosch’s own timeline in the same weeks includes “avant nft season soon bois” (https://x.com/supermetalx/status/1482820845873549315, 17 Jan 2022, 8:03am AEDT) and “avant nft starter pack” (https://x.com/supermetalx/status/1486199049111982082). The Automat paper additionally cites a 16 Jan 2022 reply by Fang, “it was bosch,” which could not be retrieved as a live post during this compilation; that citation stays with the paper until a live URL is confirmed. The paper also records an anonymous early participant questioning whether inventing the phrase and constructing the social category are the same thing, given Fang’s role in promoting Bosch as an emblem of it. Those accounts, the paper says, “need not be reconciled into a single version.”

**On the fracture.** The paper states that public allegations and internal disputes “fractured what had briefly appeared to be a unified community,” and that later participant stories often treat this period as the setting in which [[MiFella]], [[Drifella]], and the [[Fellaverse]] emerged — testimonies it says should be treated cautiously. It then describes Remilia’s social ties as more durable than the collective’s unity. This archive records that claim as the paper’s, not as a verdict. Private group chats and DMs are not quoted here.
