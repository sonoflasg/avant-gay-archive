VVV (vvv.so) is an artist-led NFT launchpad and platform built on [[Solana]] (later also used for Ethereum releases such as [[111k]]). It hosts many of the main [[Avant Gay]] collections since 2025 and also publishes texts.

*Rewritten 14/11/2026 from public sources. It replaces a May 2026 Grok draft (flagged needs-sources) that named [[Jared Madere]] as “the founder,” gave the launch as “early 2025,” and said the community had shifted to VVV “by late 2024.” The corrected account follows.*

### Founders and origins

- **Co-founders.** Le Random’s August 2026 interview introduces [[Jared Madere]] and [[fairybaby420|fairybaby]] as “co-founders of VVV,” and both bios repeat it. The Automat paper also describes fairybaby420 as VVV’s co-founder.
- **The idea.** fairybaby says VVV “started as an idea for an online magazine.” It then became an alternative to a release infrastructure “mainly dominated by the [[LaunchMyNFT]] platform.” Madere then brought in [[Wretched Worm]] and [[Miles Peyton]], “who he had been running Yeche Lange with.”
- **Relation to Yeche.** GardenParty85 writes that “In early 2025, Yeche built a launchpad for Solana NFTs called VVV.so,” and that it “is not curated.” Le Random’s timeline calls VVV “the digital arm of [[Galerie Yeche Lange]].” In Miles Peyton’s account, given in the Automat paper, VVV grew out of infrastructure first built for specific Yeche projects.

### Launch, side by side

- **The Automat paper:** “VVV formally launched on May 19, 2025, with Parker Ito’s [[Horses?2]] as its first public collection.”
- **Le Random, 10 Aug 2026:** describes *Rare Weitsmans* as VVV’s “first official release.” *Inference:* this probably means the platform’s own first release, as distinct from the artists’ collections it had hosted since May 2025. The sources do not say so.

Either way, the draft’s claim that the community had moved to VVV “by late 2024” conflicts with every launch date given. GardenParty85 and Automat both place VVV in 2025.

### Collections released on VVV (examples, with Automat register dates)

[[Horses?2]] (Parker Ito, 19 May 2025); [[Drilady]] (Parker Ito, 23 Jun 2025); [[Drifella III]] (Evil Biscuit, 4 Sep 2025), the first use of VVV’s physical-redemption feature; [[CLOUDCASTLE]] (terrorism, 15 Sep 2025); [[Heavy Liquid Graphic]] (Evil Biscuit and Parker Ito, 31 Oct 2025); [[Horses?7]] (Parker Ito); [[Marelady]] (Parker Ito, Aug 2026); and [[111k]] (Ethereum, Nov 2026). For [[Year of the Horse Part 1]], VVV built a new purchase interface. GardenParty85 also names [[Evil Biscuit]], [[Gremlins 2 Official]], [[Parker Ito]], [[Jim Spindle]] and [[Time]] among artists who released there.

### Other activity

- **Texts.** VVV publishes essays, including [[lowbie]]’s [[THERE IS NO HISTORY]] (23 Aug 2025).
- **Radio.** Le Random’s December 2025 bio says lowbie hosts WORLD RECEIVER for VVV.so.
- **$VAJ.** The Automat paper mentions a $VAJ project on which Gremlins2Movie worked with Madere. The earlier draft also linked a character, [[Vajunnie]], to the platform. No public source for that link was found in this pass.
